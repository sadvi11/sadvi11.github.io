Chunking sets the ceiling on everything downstream

Retrieval cannot return a passage that chunking never produced. Whatever you do afterwards is bounded by that.

A chunk has to carry its own context

What gets embedded and what gets displayed do not have to be the same string. Once you separate them, a whole class of retrieval failure disappears.

Why hybrid search, and how rank fusion actually works

A good embedding model cannot reliably find RTN-14. Keyword search cannot find a paraphrase. The interesting part is not running both — it is that you cannot add their scores together, and what you do instead.
